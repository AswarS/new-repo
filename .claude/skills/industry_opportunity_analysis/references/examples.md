# Examples

## Positive Trigger Examples

These user requests should trigger this skill:

1. "Analyze the opportunities and risks in AI healthcare."
2. "What roles are emerging in fintech and how can I enter?"
3. "Is the climate tech industry worth exploring now?"

## Negative Trigger Examples

These user requests should not trigger this skill:

1. "Choose my primary target from PM and analyst."
2. "Explain the daily work of a data analyst."
3. "Write an application strategy for fintech jobs."

## Ambiguous Cases

These requests may require clarification or routing:

1. "Is AI a good career?" Clarify industry scope, role area, and whether personal fit matters.
2. "What should I apply for in healthcare?" Clarify whether the user needs industry analysis or application strategy.

## Complete Example

### User

"Analyze opportunities in AI education. I have operations experience and want realistic entry roles."

### Expected Skill Behavior

Define scope, use current evidence if available, summarize trends, opportunities, risks, recommended roles, and entry suggestions with profile-fit notes.

### Expected Output Outline

Scope, assumptions, trend summary, opportunities, risks, recommended roles, entry suggestions, and handoff suggestion.

## Bad Output Example

"AI education is booming. Take an AI course and become a product manager."

### Why It Fails

It relies on hype, gives simplistic entry advice, ignores risks, and lacks evidence or role specificity.

## Good Output Outline

Discuss adoption drivers, procurement friction, regulation, role clusters, evidence uncertainty, and reversible entry actions.
