# Examples

## Positive Trigger Examples

These user requests should trigger this skill:

1. "What does a data analyst do every day?"
2. "Explain the tools, deliverables, and pressure of a product manager."
3. "What misconceptions do people have about customer success roles?"

## Negative Trigger Examples

These user requests should not trigger this skill:

1. "Pick my main target role from analyst and PM."
2. "What gaps do I have for this JD?"
3. "Design a project to prove I can do this role."

## Ambiguous Cases

These requests may require clarification or routing:

1. "Is PM right for me?" Ask whether the user wants role cognition or fit/positioning.
2. "Tell me about AI jobs." Clarify target role or route to industry opportunity analysis.

## Complete Example

### User

"I saw a business analyst job. What does this role really do?"

### Expected Skill Behavior

Ask for the JD if not provided, or give a first-pass explanation with assumptions. Cover daily tasks, capabilities, deliverables, pressure, and misunderstandings.

### Expected Output Outline

Role explanation, daily tasks, core capabilities, deliverables, misconceptions, assumptions, evidence used, and suggested next skill.

## Bad Output Example

"Business analysts write requirements and talk to stakeholders."

### Why It Fails

It is too generic and omits tools, deliverables, pressure, role boundaries, and misconceptions.

## Good Output Outline

Explain discovery, requirement analysis, process mapping, stakeholder coordination, documentation, metrics, tradeoffs, and common misconceptions.
